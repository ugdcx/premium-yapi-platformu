Audit and refine all animations.

Allowed animations:
- fade in
- slight translateY
- subtle hover opacity
- subtle line movement
- header blur transition
- mobile menu fade/slide
- calm section reveal

Forbidden animations:
- parallax
- typing effect
- bounce
- spinning loaders
- colorful animated gradients
- excessive page transitions
- infinite marquee
- dramatic zooms

Tasks:
1. Make all animations consistent.
2. Use short durations:
   - 0.2s for hover
   - 0.4s to 0.7s for reveal
3. Use easing that feels premium.
4. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
5. Disable or simplify animations for users who prefer reduced motion.
6. Ensure animations do not hurt mobile performance.

Keep the site calm and premium.
